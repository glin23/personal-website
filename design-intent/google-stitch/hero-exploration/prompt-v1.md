<!-- Component: Direction 1 — Floating 3D Object Hero -->

Design a hero section for an AI builder personal portfolio, featuring a single luminous 3D object suspended in space as the visual centerpiece.

- Center-stage abstract 3D form (liquid chrome sphere or morphing geometric shape) occupying the right two-thirds of the viewport
- Left-aligned headline "I build AI products." in oversized bold sans-serif, with subtitle "Babson '27 · Boston" underneath
- Sticky top navigation: logo wordmark and links Work, Writing, About, Contact
- Bottom-left scroll cue with thin animated line and "Scroll to explore"
- Subtle drifting particles around the 3D object
- Soft ambient glow emanating from the object, casting colored light onto the dark backdrop

Visual style: ultra-modern futurism, deep navy or charcoal background, iridescent silver-purple-teal gradients on the 3D object, generous negative space, ultra-tight kerning on display type, premium cinematic mood blending Apple product reveals with Awwwards-tier 3D portfolios.

Constraints: full-bleed hero filling 100vh, desktop-first, single accent treatment, no decorative chrome beyond the centerpiece object.

<!-- Component: Direction 2 — Explorable Low-Poly 3D World -->

Design a hero that presents a personal portfolio as a small explorable low-poly 3D world the visitor can navigate.

- Full-viewport 3D landscape: gentle hills, scattered floating islands, distinct buildings labeled with project names (Resume2Web, Semori)
- Small character or vehicle parked center-stage, ready to explore
- Floating UI panel top-left: name "Lee Lin" with subtitle "Roam to explore my work"
- Minimap in bottom-right showing the world with point-of-interest markers
- WASD keyboard hint badge near the character
- Interactive labels above each building, softly bobbing up and down

Visual style: vibrant low-poly inspired by Monument Valley and Bruno Simon, soft pastel sky gradient (peach, lilac, sky blue), cel-shaded surfaces, playful but premium, hand-crafted toy-like quality, friendly geometric typography.

Constraints: full-bleed canvas, isometric or angled third-person view, all UI overlay elements semi-transparent so the world remains focal.
